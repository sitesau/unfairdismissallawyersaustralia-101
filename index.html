What to prepare before contacting anyone in your area

The enquiry prompts ask when the dismissal took effect, the state and the stage the matter has reached. Those prompts reward preparation. A short timeline that separates confirmed facts from assumptions usually makes the first contact more productive than a long general account.

  • Note the dismissal date, the role and the employer's stated reason.
  • List documents received, such as the termination letter, contract, payslips, meeting invitations and warning letters.
  • Record the stage, including internal emails, meetings or HR responses.
  • State the outcome worth discussing, in plain terms.

Because the published terms say details may be shared with suitable professionals, only include information you are comfortable sending for that purpose. Sensitive facts can be summarised first, with fuller documents provided later once you know who will review them and how confidentiality is handled.
